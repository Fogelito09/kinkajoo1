Our Games & Apps

Rummikub

Updates coming soon

The original Rummikub (not Rummy nor Rummi or Remy) is one of the most popular family games, played by millions of people all over the world. The unique combination of tactical thinking, luck and tense competition has made this classic family game to one of the most successful games for the past 60 years! Play against friends or strangers online. Available on iOS, Android, smart TVs and internet browsers. Arrange the tiles to create the smartest color and numbers combinations. Will you be the first player to place all tiles and win the match?

Rummikub Premium

The original Rummikub Premium version. The familiar game play and rules without ads. Available in English, German, French, Dutch, Korean, Spanish, Chinese, Hebrew, Polish, Portuguese and Turkish. Play against friends or strangers online. Choose your opponents number (1-3 players) and turn time limit (30, 60, 120 seconds). Arrange the tiles to create the smartest color and numbers combinations. This version of the game is also available on ELAL planes.

TAKI

TAKI is a card game that combines an equal share of luck and strategy, which is the secret for its perfect game flow. Action cards, exciting rules and Super Taki cards will hook you right from the start and satisfy you like no other game. Play against 1-3 players.

Rummikub® Score Timer

The original Rummikub® score timer makes your Rummikub experience much more comfortable. Stop using Stoppers, pencils and papers and get a tailor made game management solution for a perfect Rummikub game.

Rummikub® Junior

Rummikub® Junior is a unique, challenging educational game based on the original Rummikub®. Rummikub® Junior develops cognitive skills including identifying numbers and colors, forming and dissolving groups, building sets of consecutive numbers, forming sets and logical reasoning.

About Us

Kinkajoo Ltd was founded by Nadav Fogel & Erez Bar in 2012 with the goal of creating fun games and apps for everyone to enjoy. After more than 20 years of experience combined in the digital casual games industry, they decided to start something fresh.

Now working with a team of talented developers, designers and game creators to make this dream come true. Based in Tel-Aviv, Israel, the self-funded, fully independent company has already developed a number of great apps including the original Rummikub®, TAKI and more.
